# RM08 — STEAM Child Safety & Age Adaptation Guide
Version: R02 preparation v0.1 | Provenance: project-prepared derivative draft | Review: owner-approved 2026-10-23; field tests not performed

## Default context
Adult-facilitated STEAM activity for children; label age recommendations as approximate and require a specific adult check for dexterity, supervision, ability and environment. In uncertain settings choose a lower-risk alternative.

## Safety matrix
| Hazard | Risk prompt | Safer default | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Button/coin cells | Can a child access/swallow it? | no loose cells; preassembled supervised low-voltage device or non-electric model | accessible loose cell |
| Small parts/scissors/pins | choking or cuts? | large pieces, child-safe scissors, adult precutting | unmanaged sharp tools/small parts |
| Heat/hot glue/flames | burns or fumes? | room-temperature adhesives, adult-only heat work | child using unguarded heat |
| Projectile/catapult/pressure | eye injury or uncontrolled launch? | non-projectile elastic-energy demonstration, soft stationary model | uncontrolled firing or aiming at people |
| Electricity | mains/current/heating risk? | no mains connections; adult-selected enclosed low-voltage kits | exposed mains/unsafe wiring |
| Height/structural loads | collapsing/falling components? | tabletop miniature with lightweight measured loads | person-bearing structures or overhead loads |
| Chemicals | irritation/toxicity? | water, paper, food-safe non-ingestion materials as suitable | unknown/unsafe mixtures |
| Digital privacy | account, location, contact risk? | local offline game and anonymous session only | unapproved data collection/social features |
| Rewards and screen time | compulsion/frustration? | bounded session, non-manipulative feedback, regular breaks | distress or unhealthy play pressure |

## Age/accessibility gate
5–8: brief sessions, large manipulatives, short instructions and adult-mediated reading; 9–12: measured iterations, more rule comparison; 13–15: multi-variable tradeoffs and reflective analysis. All bands are guides, not permissions. Offer nonvisual, motor-friendly, hearing-friendly or lower-literacy alternatives as needed.

## Mandatory adult checkpoint
Before materials are gathered, ask adult to verify local conditions, equipment, allergies, skill level and supervision. Where hazard cannot be removed, do not provide unsafe construction steps; choose a safer learning analogue. During any incident or injury, stop activity and seek appropriate local help. Digital games must not collect identifying child data or create public leaderboards by default.


## Activity-specific safety assessment (R02 review amendment; owner-approved documentation; field testing still pending)

This is a risk review, **not** a guarantee of safety. Adult must approve exact materials and local conditions before use.

### A01 — Paper Bridge Load Challenge
- Hazards: falling books, pinched fingers, collapsing bridge.
- Required safer default: Use tabletop span, light identical weights placed one at a time, no body-loading.
- Adult checkpoint: Adult stabilizes supports; keep toes/hands outside drop zone.
- Approximate suitability: 5–8 with adult; 9–15 adapted.
- Status: owner-approved safety control; not field-tested or independently certified.

### A02 — Rubber Band Powered Car
- Hazards: rubber-band snap, sharp axle ends, choking-sized wheels.
- Required safer default: Use large wheels and blunt/precut axles; low tension; no aiming.
- Adult checkpoint: Adult manages cutting and checks band condition.
- Approximate suitability: 6–8 with close adult help; 9–15 adapted.
- Status: owner-approved safety control; not field-tested or independently certified.

### A03 — Wind Powered Car
- Hazards: fan blades, loose small parts, sharp craft tools.
- Required safer default: Handheld paper fan; no exposed electric fan or sharp rotor.
- Adult checkpoint: Adult precuts and checks wheels/axles.
- Approximate suitability: 5–8 guided; 9–15 adapted.
- Status: owner-approved safety control; not field-tested or independently certified.

### A04 — Simple Circuit Night Light
- Hazards: accessible button cells, short circuit, heat, exposed wiring.
- Required safer default: Enclosed low-voltage child-rated module; prefer non-electric light model for youngest.
- Adult checkpoint: Adult selects power source; no mains, no loose button/coin cells.
- Approximate suitability: 7–8 with adult and safe kit; 9–15 with adult.
- Status: owner-approved safety control; not field-tested or independently certified.

### A05 — Paper Linkage Mechanism
- Hazards: paper fasteners, sharp pins, pinch points.
- Required safer default: Use large paper fasteners installed by adult; avoid loose metal pins.
- Adult checkpoint: Adult prepares holes and supervises fingers.
- Approximate suitability: 5–8 close adult help; 9–15 adapted.
- Status: owner-approved safety control; not field-tested or independently certified.

### A06 — Air Pressure Rocket
- Hazards: uncontrolled projectile/pressure, eye injuries.
- Required safer default: Replace with balloon-airflow demonstration tethered to line, no rocket launch.
- Adult checkpoint: Do not pressurize bottles or launch toward people.
- Approximate suitability: 5–15 adult-led safer substitute.
- Status: owner-approved safety control; not field-tested or independently certified.

### A07 — Seed Germination Observation
- Hazards: mold, allergies, seed ingestion.
- Required safer default: Use large non-toxic seeds, sealed observation container, no tasting.
- Adult checkpoint: Adult checks allergies, handwashing and mold disposal.
- Approximate suitability: 5–8 guided; 9–15 adapted.
- Status: owner-approved safety control; not field-tested or independently certified.

### A08 — Light & Shadow Projection Box
- Hazards: scissors, bright light/eye exposure, heat.
- Required safer default: Use cool battery light; never stare into bright beam; precut card.
- Adult checkpoint: Adult handles cutter and verifies cool illumination.
- Approximate suitability: 5–8 guided; 9–15 adapted.
- Status: owner-approved safety control; not field-tested or independently certified.

### A09 — Straw Tower Load Challenge
- Hazards: collapse, sharp skewer/connectors, falling weights.
- Required safer default: Use paper straw with blunt connectors and lightweight loads.
- Adult checkpoint: Adult checks small connectors and keeps tests tabletop.
- Approximate suitability: 5–8 with large pieces; 9–15 adapted.
- Status: owner-approved safety control; not field-tested or independently certified.

### A10 — Elastic Catapult
- Hazards: launched objects and eye injuries, elastic snap.
- Required safer default: Substitute lever with fixed stop and non-launching soft load.
- Adult checkpoint: Do not provide instructions for free-flight projectiles in child use.
- Approximate suitability: 5–15 adult-led non-projectile substitute.
- Status: owner-approved safety control; not field-tested or independently certified.

### A11 — Egg Drop Protection Challenge
- Hazards: falling objects, broken egg contamination, height exposure.
- Required safer default: Use plastic imitation egg at table height; drop into tray from short height.
- Adult checkpoint: Adult manages cleanup; no balcony/stair/window drops.
- Approximate suitability: 5–8 close adult help; 9–15 adapted.
- Status: owner-approved safety control; not field-tested or independently certified.

### A12 — Friction Ramp Investigation
- Hazards: rolling parts, fingers, trip hazard.
- Required safer default: Small tabletop ramp with light large rolling object and capture stop.
- Adult checkpoint: Adult clamps/stabilizes ramp; clear path.
- Approximate suitability: 5–8 guided; 9–15 adapted.
- Status: owner-approved safety control; not field-tested or independently certified.

### A13 — Paper Airplane Glide Ratio Lab
- Hazards: eye strike, unsafe elevated throws.
- Required safer default: Throw soft paper plane toward open wall away from people, from floor level.
- Adult checkpoint: Adult selects clear indoor test zone; no elevated ledges.
- Approximate suitability: 5–8 guided; 9–15 adapted.
- Status: owner-approved safety control; not field-tested or independently certified.

### A14 — Polygon Stability Tower
- Hazards: topple and falling weights, sharp tools.
- Required safer default: Tabletop lightweight tower and low-mass test loads.
- Adult checkpoint: Adult limits height/weight, supervises collapse zone.
- Approximate suitability: 5–8 guided; 9–15 adapted.
- Status: owner-approved safety control; not field-tested or independently certified.

### A15 — Solar Box Heater
- Hazards: hot surfaces, sun glare, glass, food safety.
- Required safer default: Measure warmth only; no food preparation; no reflective concentration toward faces.
- Adult checkpoint: Adult handles setup and thermometer; stop if hot to touch.
- Approximate suitability: 9–15 adult supervised; 5–8 non-heating alternative.
- Status: owner-approved safety control; not field-tested or independently certified.
