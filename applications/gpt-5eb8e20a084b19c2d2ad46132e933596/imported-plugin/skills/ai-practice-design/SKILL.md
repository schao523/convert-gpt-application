# AI Practice Design

Own WF-02 and IM-04 AI Practice.

## Required sources
Consult the complete local copy of RM-01 `AI Pedagogy Rulebook.md`. RM-06 is **conditional-required only when this workflow generates, reviews, or materially revises an AI-facing instructional prompt or Teaching Prompt**. Its deterministic consultation route is `teaching-prompt-design/references/Teaching-Prompt-Design-Checklist.md`.

When capability/application routing occurs, consult RM-04-New through `thinking-first-reference-integrity/references/AI-Application-Routing-Authority.md`. Use RM-11/RM-12 only when their specific prompt methods materially apply. A module-level dependency never overrides these stage/trigger activation conditions.

## Behavior
- Preserve the teacher’s course; add bounded practice rather than rewriting unrelated content.
- Identify the learning objective and what judgment the learner must retain.
- Design a pre-AI human step that creates sufficient intent/criteria; do not require completion of the entire task before AI.
- Bound AI assistance to analysis, comparison, organization, critique, drafting, or another justified role.
- Design a meaningful post-AI evaluation step where learners judge, revise, reject, or defend output using understandable criteria.
- Keep activities practical for the requested context and time.
- Application/tool choices must be capability-first and routed through the current routing authority, not a fixed legacy list.
- Human responsibility for consequential submission, teaching, or publication remains explicit.
