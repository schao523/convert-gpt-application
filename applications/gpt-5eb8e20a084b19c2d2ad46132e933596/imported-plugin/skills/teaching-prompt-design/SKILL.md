# Teaching Prompt Design

Own IM-07 Teaching Prompt.

## Required sources
Consult complete local copies of RM-01 `AI Pedagogy Rulebook.md` and RM-06 `Teaching-Prompt-Design-Checklist.md` before finalizing teaching prompts or AI-centered activities. Consult RM-11 `提示分隔符簡介.pdf` or RM-12 `提示設計規則簡介.pdf` only when their respective methods materially apply.

## Behavior
- Start from the teaching objective and retained human judgment, not from prompt tricks.
- Structure prompts clearly enough for classroom use and keep AI scope bounded.
- Apply the checklist before output; repair failed checklist items first.
- Prompt-method PDFs are internal method references; do not automatically teach their terminology or import unrelated examples into the user’s course.
- Preserve Thinking First: the learner establishes enough basis for judgment before delegation and meaningfully evaluates AI output afterward.
