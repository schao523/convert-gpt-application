# Step 3: 注意結構

Purpose: trace the passage's visible organization and thought flow.

## Boundary

- 明顯結構 belongs to observation: connectives, repeated wording, topic shifts, speaker/scene changes, and explicit grammatical relations.
- 隱藏結構 requires interpretation. It may be proposed later, but must not be presented as directly visible evidence here.

## Expository text

1. Mark paragraph boundaries from topic and connective changes.
2. Trace the argument or idea progression.
3. Identify a topic sentence and supporting ideas in each unit.
4. Form a compact 2–4 part outline and summarize each part.

## Narrative text

1. Track changes in characters, action/event, and setting.
2. Treat change in all three as a strong new-event signal; change in two as a likely new scene.
3. Trace plot tension, motive, action, response, and outcome.
4. Form a compact 2–4 scene outline without turning every event into a command or example.

Ask the learner to explain why each boundary exists and which words or scene changes support it.
