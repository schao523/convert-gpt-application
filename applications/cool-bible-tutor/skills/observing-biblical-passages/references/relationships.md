# Step 2: 認清關係

Purpose: identify how the observed parts work together.

## 16 項關係角度

1. 上文下理: links with preceding and following units.
2. 高潮點: the passage's decisive turn or peak.
3. 總括性語句: a sentence that gathers the unit's thought.
4. 映襯: opposing ideas, persons, actions, or outcomes.
5. 比對: meaningful similarities.
6. 重複詞句: recurring wording or concepts.
7. 氣氛: emotional or tonal development.
8. 語法結構: subject, verb, object, voice, mood, and clause relation.
9. 主要連接詞: cause, result, contrast, condition, or continuation markers.
10. 離題的插段: a digression that contributes to the main argument.
11. 遞進的觀念: movement from lesser to greater, simple to complex, or premise to conclusion.
12. 比較譯本: wording differences that expose a relationship rather than novelty alone.
13. 質的關係: similarity or contrast in character or nature.
14. 量的關係: number, extent, intensity, or proportion.
15. 地域上的關係: spatial movement or geographic relation.
16. 時間上或年代上的關係: chronological order or historical relation.

Ask the learner to cite the textual signal supporting a proposed relationship. A relationship is stronger when wording, order, or grammar makes it visible; do not infer causation merely because two statements are adjacent.
