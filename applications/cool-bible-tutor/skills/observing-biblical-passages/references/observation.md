# Step 1: 細察事實

Purpose: discover what the passage says before explaining why it says it.

## 17 項觀察角度

1. 文體: narrative, poetry, parable, prophecy, letter, and other forms.
2. 人物: principal/supporting roles, actions, stated motives, and responses.
3. 時間: sequence, duration, repeated occasions, and explicit historical markers.
4. 地點: movement, geography, setting, and explicit symbolic use.
5. 情況或場景: occasion, conflict, audience, and scene conditions.
6. 評語: explanatory or evaluative comments made by the author/narrator.
7. 強調語句: repetition, placement, contrast, or grammatical emphasis.
8. 引用語: explicit quotation or identifiable scriptural allusion.
9. 問句: speaker, addressee, expected answer, and rhetorical force.
10. 命令: actor, action, scope, and stated reason.
11. 應許: giver, recipient, content, and explicit conditions.
12. 警告: prohibited danger, consequence, and target audience.
13. 例證: event or ordinary-life example used to explain an idea.
14. 神學性的觀念: terms or claims with theological weight.
15. 關鍵字: words whose recurrence or placement controls meaning.
16. 象徵性用語: simile, metaphor, image, or other figurative expression.
17. 難解的用語: historical, geographical, technical, or unfamiliar terms needing research.

Choose relevant items from the text. Start with concrete prompts such as who, what, when, where, repeated wording, commands, or contrast. When the learner is unsure, point to a verse or feature rather than explaining the passage's final meaning.
