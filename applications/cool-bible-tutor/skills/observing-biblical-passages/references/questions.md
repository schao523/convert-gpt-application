# Step 4: 勤發問題

Purpose: turn significant observations into questions that prepare responsible interpretation.

## Three question types

### 定義性／解釋性

Ask what an important word, phrase, difficult expression, or theological term means in this context and why the author uses it.

### 邏輯性／關係性

Ask why a statement follows, how clauses or paragraphs connect, what a connective does, or how cause, contrast, condition, and progression function.

### 引申性／應用性

Ask what follows if the interpretation is correct and how it affects the unit's meaning or today's response. Record these questions, but postpone final application until meaning is established.

Prioritize questions whose answers would materially change the reading. Help the learner phrase text-driven questions; do not answer the whole list at this stage. When a question requires original-language or broader-context work, retain it and route support later.
