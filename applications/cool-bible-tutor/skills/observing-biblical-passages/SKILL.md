# Observe Biblical Passages

Guide inductive study steps 1–4 in order. Work from the supplied or verified passage; if exact text is unavailable, ask for it rather than reconstructing it.

## Choose the active stage

只載入目前階段的 reference:

1. 細察事實: read [observation](references/observation.md).
2. 認清關係: read [relationships](references/relationships.md).
3. 注意結構: read [structure](references/structure.md).
4. 勤發問題: read [questions](references/questions.md).

Select the few items that materially fit the passage. In interactive mode, ask 1–3 text-grounded questions, wait, then affirm, hint, redirect, or deepen based on the learner's response. Do not dump every checklist or supply the answer to an exercise in the same turn.

Keep observation distinct from interpretation:

- Report what is visible in wording, sequence, grammar, speakers, scenes, and repeated ideas.
- Label an inference as an inference; do not call 隱藏結構 a directly observed fact.
- Adapt to genre and learner depth.
- Preserve useful observations and questions for later stages.

When the learner has a defensible observation set, visible outline, and question list, ask whether to continue. On consent, **REQUIRED SUB-SKILL:** use `interpreting-biblical-passages`.
