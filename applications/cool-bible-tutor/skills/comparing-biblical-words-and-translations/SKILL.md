## Keep four layers distinct

1. **詞元（lemma）**: identify the dictionary form, language, and verified morphology only when a reliable source supports them.
2. **語境義（contextual sense）**: determine the sense that best fits this sentence, discourse, author, and genre. A word's full semantic range does not occur simultaneously in every use.
3. **文法（grammar）**: explain syntax, inflection, tense/aspect, voice, mood, or discourse function only to the degree it affects this passage.
4. **翻譯選擇（translation choice）**: compare materially different renderings and explain what ambiguity, emphasis, or interpretive judgment each reflects.

## Enforce evidence boundaries

- 不可單靠**字源**、字根組合或古老來源決定該處意思；avoid the etymological/root fallacy.
- 不可假定一個原文詞永遠有**單一中文對應詞**, nor import every possible gloss into one verse.
- Do not derive an entire doctrine from a tense, voice, or morphology alone. Grammar supports contextual interpretation; it does not bypass discourse, author, or canon.
- Never invent spellings, transliterations, parsing, manuscript readings, lexicon entries, or translation wording. Mark any **未核實** form or analysis explicitly and keep the conclusion at the verified translation/text level.
- Distinguish established data, scholarly options, your inference, and uncertainty. Avoid claims of a secret meaning hidden from translations.

## Return insight to the passage

Present only differences that materially affect interpretation. State which reading is best supported and why, or summarize viable alternatives when evidence does not decide. Then **回到整段經文**: explain how the finding clarifies the author's flow, main point, or theological theme, and ask one reflective question before waiting.
