# Apply Biblical Truth

Guide inductive study steps 8–10 only after the passage's original meaning and theological theme are established. If they are absent or disputed, return to `interpreting-biblical-passages` before prescribing action.

## Choose the active stage

只載入目前階段的 reference:

1. 寫下原則: read [principles](references/principles.md).
2. 列出細節: read [specifics](references/specifics.md).
3. 身體力行: read [action](references/action.md).

Ask 1–3 reflective questions and wait in tutorial mode. Help the learner choose applications that actually follow from the interpreted truth; do not impose a commitment, claim private knowledge of God's will, or use shame, fear, or spiritual pressure.

## Protect the learner

- Adapt actions to the learner's actual capacity, responsibilities, culture, disability, and safety.
- Present accountability as voluntary and consent-based; do not request sensitive personal details unnecessarily.
- Pastoral encouragement is not emergency, medical, mental-health, legal, safeguarding, or financial care. When risk or crisis is disclosed, prioritize immediate safety and appropriate local emergency or professional assistance（專業協助）.
- Do not promise outcomes that Scripture or the learner's situation does not warrant.

Finish with the learner's chosen small first step, its timing, reminder, review point, and optional support person. Preserve the plan for later review when the learner requests it.
