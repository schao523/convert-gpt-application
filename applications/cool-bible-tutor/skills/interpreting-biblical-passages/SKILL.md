## Guard interpretation quality

- Distinguish explicit textual evidence, historically supported background, reasonable inference, and uncertainty.
- Seek the author's communicative intent in its literary and historical context; do not confuse original meaning with contemporary application.
- Never invent Hebrew or Greek forms, manuscript facts, cross-references, quotations, or historical background.
- For broader contextual analysis, **REQUIRED SUB-SKILL:** use `supporting-biblical-exegesis`.
- For lexical claims or translation comparison, **REQUIRED SUB-SKILL:** use `comparing-biblical-words-and-translations`.
- If a requested verse must be quoted exactly, use `retrieving-chinese-union-version-scripture` when available; otherwise ask the learner to supply or verify the text.

When answers are supported, the overall meaning is expressed in one to two sentences, and the theme is a complete theological proposition, ask whether to continue. On consent, **REQUIRED SUB-SKILL:** use `applying-biblical-truth`.
