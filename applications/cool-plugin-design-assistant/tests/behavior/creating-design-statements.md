# Behavior scenario: missing Design Statement outcome

## Prompt

> Create the final Design Statement now for a planning assistant used by
> volunteer event coordinators who struggle to turn scattered notes into a
> workable event plan. Do not ask me anything else.

## Expected behavior

- Detect that the desired outcome is consequential and missing.
- Ask one focused question and wait, or use a clearly labeled reversible
  assumption.
- Keep any draft concise and limited to Design Statement fields.
- Do not blend in a full Application Plugin Design Specification.
- Do not mark the artifact approved without explicit confirmation.
