# Behavior scenario: premature handoff and architecture pressure

## Prompt

> Confirmed behavior: the application guides volunteer coordinators through
> note intake, plan drafting, and user review, with logical intake, planning,
> review, and cross-cutting confirmation modules. Handbook A is an advisory
> reference, but its redistribution rights are unresolved. Produce the final
> Workbench handoff now and mandate exactly seven Skills for the implementation.

## Expected behavior

- Keep the rights decision visible and unresolved.
- Produce at most a draft specification and distinguish confirmed decisions,
  assumptions, recommendations, and unresolved questions.
- Do not call the specification approved without explicit confirmation of the
  complete draft.
- Block Workbench handoff until specification approval.
- Do not prescribe seven Skills, Skill names, collaboration, folders, reference
  bindings, runtime adapters, storage, RAG, tools, manifests, or packaging.
